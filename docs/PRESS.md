# Press resources

The official [Kodo press kit](https://www.ttstudio.net/en/kodo-press.html) contains:

- short and long product descriptions
- current feature and privacy information
- App Store and product links
- downloadable media assets
- contact information

Media assets may be used for editorial coverage of Kodo. For interviews, technical questions or additional assets, contact [info@ttstudio.net](mailto:info@ttstudio.net).
